📖 How to Play Least Count Rush

Official Rules & Scoring Guide

🎯 The Objective

Finish each round with the lowest card points. Scores accumulate over rounds until players exceed the elimination threshold (e.g. 150 pts). The last survivor wins!

🃏 Card Point Values

0 pts Wilds & Jokers
1 pt Aces (All suits)
2–9 pts Face values (2-9)
10 pts 10, J, Q, K (or 10-13 Classic)

🔄 Turn Flow: Drop & Draw

  1. Select & Drop: Select 1 card or multiple cards of the same rank (e.g. two 8s) and place them into the Middle Drop Zone.
  2. Change Mind: Tap any other card in your hand to swap it into the drop zone before committing.
  3. Draw to Finish: Tap either the Left Closed Deck or Right Open Discard Pile to draw 1 card. Your dropped card becomes the top discard for the next player!

⚡ 1-Tap SLASH (The Rush Move)

When the previous player drops a card that matches a card in your hand, tap ⚡ SLASH! You drop your matching card(s) without drawing any replacement, shrinking your hand immediately!

👑 Calling SHOW (Declaration)

  • Only permitted when your hand total is 10 points or below before dropping cards.
  • Good Show: If your hand is strictly lowest at the table, you score 0 points! Other players score their full hand points.
  • Bad Show: If anyone ties or beats your score, you take a heavy +40 penalty points!
